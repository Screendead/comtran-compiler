The sum-to-7 constraint is one equation; any hybrid with one free tail-affecting weight can satisfy it (e.g. counting each PI recomputation as its 2 emitted operators yields (3,2,3,2,4,1) = 15 exactly), but every such weight is tuned against the very sum it predicts and is internally inconsistent with the exemption that saves statement 207, so it is a fit, not a derivation. The manuals define no counting basis: J 90.02.03 states only 'N is the sum of maximum Result Storage used in each section', and the only other 'result storage' prose in either manual (J 90.03.05) is the loader's load-time complex-expression evaluator — a fixed roster of cells 0-6 with terminator B=7, a different mechanism sharing the name. The tail split of 7 across sections 3-5 is unobservable from this sample, and the counting rule behind 'maximum used' is unrecoverable from it. This is the outcome the review record's item 2 planned for: the answer returns with the search recorded.
